NOTES FOR YOUR LCA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Two design files in WT_Ontology/ match your two variations exactly:
    IEA-15-240-RWT.yaml               = monopile / fixed-bottom base
    IEA-15-240-RWT_VolturnUS-S.yaml   = floating (semi-sub)
- The generator is direct-drive: there is NO gearbox (gearbox mass = 0,
  hss mass = 0). If your BOM lists a gearbox, remove it.
- Generator rotor + stator = 368,839. Enter them as two items OR one -
  never both the two parts AND the 368,839 total, or you double-count
  ~369 t.
- Blades are per-turbine 3 x 67,920. If your process is per-blade use
  67,920; per-turbine use 203,763.
- Seawater fluid ballast (floater) is not a purchased/manufactured
  material - keep it out of material demand and CO2, or your floater
  numbers inflate by ~9,500 t.
